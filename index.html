Before you ask

Ques­tions

Do I have to install anything?

No app and no login. We text you a link, you tap three buttons, and your phone does the rest. It takes about a minute.

Do I keep my number?

Yes. Calls ring you first, like they always have. Only the ones you don't answer get passed on.

Will customers know it's automated?

Yes. The first text says it's your business's automated assistant and that you've missed their call. It never pretends to be you.

Does it quote prices or book jobs?

No. It collects the details and tells them you'll ring back. What you charge and when you're free is your call.

What if someone rings from a landline?

The text only works on mobiles. A landline caller won't get one, and you'll still see the missed call on your phone as normal.

What about emergencies?

If someone mentions a gas smell or flooding, it tells them to ring 999 or the gas emergency line, and flags the job to you as urgent straight away.

Will it work on my phone?

It uses the call forwarding built into UK mobile networks. We check it works on your phone when we set you up.

What if I want to stop?

Tap one button and calls go back to normal. No contract and no notice period.
